# Area transitions and the 16→7 loop — the full-game progression, trace-confirmed

- Mechanic: AREA-04 (present) — areas advance 1→2→…→16 and then continue at area 7, with no win screen, for as long as the player survives. The advance mechanism itself was built and unit-tested in AREA-01 (record 015); with all 16 schedules now ingested (AREA-03, record 017) the full-game loop is data-complete and confirmed by the accelerated trace.
- Derived behavior: Completing an area (the scroll clock reaching row 0x0E) advances the area number and re-tops the next area's clock, schedule cursor, and terrain column; completing area 16 sets the area number to 7 (not 1, and never a victory/end screen), so play loops through areas 7–16 indefinitely. The near-end death checkpoint (AREA-01) advances the area number the same way. Every area visited on that path has its own committed normal schedule (AREA-03), so the progression consumes real per-area content end to end.
