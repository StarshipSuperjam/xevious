# Presentation fidelity — the arcade's sound cues and its explosion, bomb, crosshair and bullet animation

- Mechanic: CAB-05 (gameplay audio and feedback), slice 20 `presentation.audio-animation`. Every remaining arcade sound cue now plays at its arcade play point and ends inside its state window, and the explosions, the bomb, the crosshair and the enemy bullets draw the arcade's own sprite frames, colours and flips at the arcade's counts. New cues: the coin sound, the two high-score entry tunes, and the Andor Genesis drone. Replaced: the base project's start jingle, flight music, death sound, shot sound and both bomb sounds give way to the arcade recordings (`start`, `bgm`, `solvalou_explode`, `zapper_fire`, one `blaster_fire`). The attract cycle is silent, as the arcade's is, and no demo starts while a credit is banked. READY now holds 32 ticks (was 30), sized to the arcade's 64-frame forest wait after a death. The text-sharpness fix carried from [record 054](054-arcade-screen-proportions.md) deviation 16 lands in the same PR and is recorded there.
- Derived behavior: (1) **Coin.** A coin that actually banks a credit plays the coin sound; at the 99 cap neither the credit nor the sound happens. It is the one sound that breaks through the attract silence. (2) **Attract silence.** The arcade mutes every sound when it enters its attract cycle with no credit banked and unmutes when a game is coined up, so the title, the demos and the best-five table are silent; the flight tune, the death cue and the start theme are also never requested in a demo. With a credit banked the arcade leaves the attract cycle altogether and waits for START, so a demo never plays with a credit in. (3) **High-score entry.** Opening initials entry plays a looping tune — the top-score tune for a new first place, the high-score tune for any lower rank — until entry ends, by finishing or by its timeout. (4) **Start theme and flight tune.** Every life of a real game opens with the main theme. The game code asks for the flight tune from the same moment, every frame of the life; that the flight tune follows the theme, rather than sounding with it, comes from the Amiga layer's sound lock, not the game code — see deviation (5). (5) **Death.** At a craft death the arcade stops the flight tune and plays the death cue (1.81 s). Nothing else stops it: it plays out through the explosion (56 frames), the 32-frame pause and the 64-frame forest wait, and ends before the next life's theme. The forest wait runs after the last death too, so the cue also ends before initials entry or GAME OVER. (6) **Shot and bomb.** Each Zapper shot plays the shot sound; each bomb drop plays the one bomb sound. (7) **Andor drone.** The boss requests its drone every frame it descends, hovers or leaves, and never once it is destroyed. (8) **Player explosion.** Seven sprite codes — C0, C1, then C4 and C8 at double size, C2, C3, then CC at double size — eight frames each, step n at colour 0x30+n, the flip taken from the frame counter (a new flip state every 4 frames), the double-size steps nudged half their overhang so every frame shares one centre; then the craft is not drawn for the 32-frame pause, nor after it on the last life, so nothing of the explosion stays over GAME OVER. (9) **Air explosion.** A struck flying enemy shows codes 70 and 71, then 74, 78 and 7C at double size, at colour 7, four frames each over its 20-frame explosion, re-centred by one cell when it grows, with a new flip state every frame. (10) **Ground explosion and crater.** A bombed ground object shows codes 60 and 61, then 64, 68 and 6C at double size, then 62 and 63, at colour 0x0C, eight frames each; then the crater flickers between codes A6 and A7 at colour 0x0D every 4 frames for as long as it is on screen. Garu nodes and water objects explode through the same codes at 4 frames each and then vanish, with no crater. The Andor Genesis gun ports and core explode through the same seven codes but step every 4 frames, so their burst lasts 28 frames, and the core turns into the Bragza the moment that burst ends. (11) **Bomb.** The bomb draws from the second graphics bank: code 0x11C, then 0x11D at frame 8 and 0x11E at frame 16, holding there, its colour stepping through 0x25–0x28 every 4 frames. The bomb target is tile 0x114 at colour 0x22. (12) **Crosshair.** Tile 0x114 at colour 32, or 33 while a bomb is in flight, plus 9 (41 or 42) on the frames where `countup & 4` is set and a live ground object (slots 2–15) sits inside the crosshair's bomb box. (13) **Enemy bullets.** Tile 0x11E, every bullet in one shared colour `0x25 + ((countup >> 1) & 3)` — a new colour every two frames — held while the scroll is stopped. The eight-entry two-palette tables beside that routine set the shared pulsing colours other objects read; they are not the bullets'.

- Scratch interpretation: **One sound owner.** Every game sound is a Stage sound, attached by `tools/hud_glyphs.py` (`render_game_sounds`), so the Stage's volume is the one mute and its stop-all the one cut. Cues raised on sprites or clones reach the Stage by relay broadcast — `sfx shot` (from the blaster) plays `zapper_fire`, `sfx death` (from `solv_death`) plays `solvalou_explode`, on the existing `sfx bacura` pattern — and cues raised in Stage-thread procs play directly (`blaster_fire` in the bomb arm, `andor_genesis` in `update andor master`). **Coin.** The warp coin poll raises `coin sound` on a below-cap add; the non-warp Stage coin loop plays `credit` after any coin-abort transition (whose stop-all would cut it) and once `audio muted` reads 0. **No demo with a credit.** The title and best-five holds launch their demo only if the hold is still current and `credits`=0 (`_attract_demo_launch`); with a credit the title stays up until START. **Attract mute.** A separate Stage green-flag loop sets the volume to 0 while `attract`=1 and `credits`=0, and back to 100 otherwise, writing `audio muted` only on a change (a volume set yields the thread). **Entry tune.** On `director enter` into initials entry the Stage loops `name_entry_top` (entry row 1) or `name_entry` with play-until-done until the state leaves entry; every exit runs a transition whose stop-all ends it. **Music.** On `director enter` into `playing` with `attract`=0 the Stage plays `start` until done, then loops `bgm` until the state leaves `playing`. **Death.** The transition into `player-dead` stops all sounds (ending the flight tune where the arcade stops it); `solv_death` then relays `sfx death` when `attract`=0. The death-complete handler sets `keep sounds`, and the one transition it runs (respawn, initials entry or GAME OVER) skips every stop-all-sounds — the transition's own and the `director stop` receivers' — and clears the flag after its stop phase. The Stage's `sfx death` receiver also holds `death cue playing` at 1 across its play-until-done of `solvalou_explode`, and every stop-all is gated on both flags, so no later stop-all — the respawning → playing edge, or the exit from initials entry — cuts the cue while it is still sounding. **Andor drone.** `andor drone timer` counts down on the boss's alive lifecycle branch; at 0 it plays `andor_genesis` and re-arms to `ANDOR_DRONE_TICKS` (53). A core death leaves that branch, so re-arming stops and the last sample plays out; `_ground_seed_andor` zeroes the timer so every arrival sounds at once. **READY.** `READY_HOLD_TICKS` = 32, on the first life and on every respawn. **Effects art.** `tools/effects_sprite_render.py` decodes the frames into one 224×128 sheet (`--verify` re-derives it byte-for-byte); every explosion frame is centred in a 32-px cell, so the double-size nudges and recentres are carried by the art and the build needs no per-frame offset; the `effects` entries in `assets/sprite-extraction/manifest.json` crop the frames and flip-expand the player and air explosions into four costumes each (none/x/y/xy). **Player explosion** (`solv_death`): each tick draws `player-explosion/burst/<step>/<flip>`, step = tick ÷ 4 over 7 steps (28 ticks), flip from the tick every 2 ticks (`PLAYER_EXPLOSION_FLIP_TICKS`), then `hide` for the 16-tick pause; the game-over route also ends hidden (it used to leave the last frame showing); drawn at the shared sprite size. **Air explosion:** every air family's explosion branch draws `air-explosion/burst/<phase>/<flip>`, phase = timer ÷ 4 frames, flip from the tick. **Andor burst:** the gun ports and core draw the ground-explosion frames every `ANDOR_EXPLOSION_PHASE_FRAMES` 4 frames (7 frames, 14 ticks); the core's Bragza conversion follows that burst. **Ground explosion:** `GROUND_EXPLOSION_PHASE_FRAMES` 8 × 7 frames, then the crater alternating every `GROUND_CRATER_FLICKER_FRAMES` 4. **Bomb:** `bomb/fall/<code>/c25..c28` by the bomb's age — the code steps every 8 frames up to the third and holds, the colour steps every 4. **Crosshair:** `crosshair/aim/{idle, bombing, idle-lit, bombing-lit}` — bombing reads `bomb in flight`, lit is `floor(tick ÷ 2)` odd and a live ground object 2–15 inside the crosshair's bomb box (`crosshair lit`, computed in `track crosshair`). **Enemy bullets:** `bomb/fall/03/c25..c28` by `tick mod 4`, at the shared sprite size (was a Toroid frame at 50%).

- Known deviations or uncertainty: (1) **One global stop at death.** The arcade stops only the flight tune (`update_solvalou` 2026); Scratch can only stop every sound at once, so the transition into `player-dead` also ends any one-shot still sounding on that tick and the Andor drone's tail. (2) **The death cue is shielded, and may overlap what follows (port only, operator decision 2026-10-05).** The death cue (1.81 s) outlasts the 44-tick explosion and pause, so the one transition after a death skips its stop-alls (`keep sounds`), and every later stop-all is also skipped while the cue is still sounding (`death cue playing`). The arcade needs neither: its cue ends inside the 64-frame forest wait, which runs after every death, the last one included (`src/xevious_main.68k` `main_gameplay_loop` 534–536, then 544–546). The port has that wait only as the READY hold on a respawn, and not at all before initials entry or GAME OVER; and its post-death pause and READY hold are empty-loop holds that a browser can run almost at once when nothing else on screen redraws. So in the port the cue's tail can overlap the next life's start theme, or the opening of the entry tune or GAME OVER after the last life, where in the arcade it never does. The missing wait before entry and GAME OVER is pre-existing and not changed here. (3) **Bomb sound plays out (port reading).** The game code stops the bomb sound on landing (`check_bomb_finished` 2508–2509); the port lets it play out, about 0.16 s past a 35-frame flight, because a stop-all would cut every other cue. The reference's Amiga layer does the same (`src/amiga/amiga.68k` `osd_sound_stop` 804–808). (4) **Andor drone period (port reading, operator decision 2026-10-04).** The game requests the drone every frame (`src/xevious_main.68k` `handle_4B_Andor_Genesis_obj_15` 5392, 5404); Scratch cannot ask whether a sound is still playing and a replay restarts it, so the port replays it every 53 ticks, the sample's own length, for a back-to-back hum. The Amiga layer restarts it every 51 calls (`src/amiga/amiga.68k` `osd_sound_start` 752–760), platform tuning. (5) **Theme lock not adopted.** The Amiga layer locks out other sounds while the main theme plays (`osd_sound_start` 726–727, 761–767); the port lets shots, bombs and hits sound over the theme and starts the flight tune only after the theme finishes, the Amiga's audible order without the lock. The game code itself asks for the flight tune from the start of the life (`main_fn_1__handle_solvalou` 2009), so whether a real cabinet sounds the two together or one after the other is not settled by this source. (6) **Full flips (operator decision 2026-10-04).** The game code sets flip bits on every explosion frame, but the Amiga renderer drops them on 2×2 sprites (`sprite_draw_double_width_and_height` 2529–2544) and mirrors only one axis on 1×1 sprites (`draw_sprite_part` 2614–2620). The port draws full horizontal and vertical flips as the Neo Geo renderer does (`src/neogeo/neogeo.68k` `update_sprite_shadow` 916–961), realizing the game's intent — a recorded divergence from the Amiga, the same call as the Andor core flip ([record 048](048-andor-core-destruction.md)). (7) **Per-tick sampling.** The port draws once per tick (two arcade frames): the air explosion's every-frame flip shows every other flip state, and the player-explosion flip and crosshair blink are counted from the port's tick, so their phase against the arcade's free-running frame counter is arbitrary. (8) **Silent attract reverses an earlier choice.** [Record 049](049-cabinet-attract.md) deviation 3 kept the flight music in the demo; the arcade mutes the whole attract cycle (`src/xevious_main.68k` `main_thread_main_loop` 355–356), so the Stage is now muted while `attract`=1 and `credits`=0 (operator decision 2026-10-04). (9) **Coin sound timing.** The coin sound plays after the coin-abort transition and after the unmute; in a browser a volume set waits about a tick, so the coin sounds a tick or two after the key press. (10) **READY 30 → 32 ticks (port reading).** The READY beat was a project-defined 30 ticks ([record 003](003-game-director-and-state-reset.md)); it is now 32, sized to the arcade's 64-frame forest wait (`main_gameplay_loop` 511, 535–536). That wait runs only after a death (the scroll is stopped only then, `finish_solvalou_exploding` 2087) and shows the reset forest with no craft; the arcade's first life has no wait, and its READY text stays up for 160 frames while play is already running (`main_gameplay_loop` 500–506, `display_player_start_msgs` 698). The port keeps its READY hold on the first life as well, and shows the craft during it, as before; only the length changed. (11) **Crosshair lit test reads last tick's positions.** The crosshair's ground-object check runs in `track crosshair` before the ground walk moves the objects this tick, a one-tick lag. (12) **Bullet pulse needs no scroll freeze.** The arcade holds the pulse while the scroll is disabled (`src/xevious_sub.68k` `sub_fn_5__handle_pulsing_colours` 209–210), which happens only outside play; the port's bullets draw only while `playing`, so the plain tick-driven pulse matches. (13) **Preserved baseline kept unplayed.** The replaced baseline sounds (`Game Start.mp3`, `BGM.mp3`, the player-death, blaster and bomb sounds) and the replaced explosion, crater, crosshair, bomb and bullet costumes stay in the project as preserved history; a test asserts no block plays or selects them. (14) **Brag Spario explosion (pre-existing, not changed here).** The arcade's Brag Spario vanishes without an explosion animation; the port's has always shown the shared air explosion, which this slice only re-arts. Left for a follow-up. (15) **Andor boss burst shortened to the arcade's.** The Andor gun ports and core used to share the 8-frame, eight-costume ground burst (64 frames, 32 ticks); with the arcade's own frames they now step every 4 frames as the arcade does (`gun_port_explosion` 5715–5741 in `src/xevious_main.68k`: 28 frames, 14 ticks). This is gameplay as well as looks: the core turns into the Bragza 18 ticks sooner, and a port caught in the chain explosion frees its slot sooner. It corrects [record 048](048-andor-core-destruction.md) deviation (1). (16) **Sample lengths measured, not cited.** The window fits (death 1.806 s, bomb 0.748 s, drone 1.773 s) rest on the staged recordings' measured lengths, and the harness cannot hear durations (play-until-done does not wait there); the operator playtest is the check. (17) **No demo with a credit banked (corrects a pre-existing gap, operator decision 2026-10-05).** The title and best-five holds used to launch their demos whatever the credit count, so after a coin, or after a game that ended with credits left, a demo ran — and with sound, since the mute applies only with no credit. The arcade goes to coined_up whenever a credit is banked and runs no demo then (`main_thread_main_loop` 353–354); the holds now launch a demo only at 0 credits. This narrows [record 049](049-cabinet-attract.md)'s attract cycle to the uncredited cabinet.
