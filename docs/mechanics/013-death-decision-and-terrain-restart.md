# Player death decision and terrain-restart-on-death

- Mechanic: PLY-02 (partial) — player death routes through the life economy (respawn while craft remain, else game over) and a new life restarts the current area from its top.
- Derived behavior: When the craft dies, the explosion plays; the game then decides from the remaining craft — a craft left respawns the craft at the fixed spawn point, immediately vulnerable; none left ends the game. On a respawn, the terrain restarts the current area from its top rather than resuming where it was.

The collision hit windows that a real trigger will use are recorded in [008](008-enemy-bullet-foundation.md) (`check_solvalou_hit` 2182-2237). The new-life-restarts-the-area rule is the settled [area progression and terrain](../spec/area-progression-and-terrain.md) reset-scope rule; the death/respawn contract is settled [player craft and weapons](../spec/player-craft-and-weapons.md) PLY-02.
- Transfer class: General behavior.
- Scratch interpretation: The D and G keys are stand-in death **triggers** (a real collision needs an attacker, which arrives in slice 8): D loses one craft, G drains the craft to the terminal life so the game-over path is reachable in one press. Neither hardcodes the outcome. The Stage `death complete` handler decides from the `craft` counter — `craft` > 0 transitions to `respawning`/`new-life`, otherwise to `game-over`/`game-over` — and sets `death outcome` to record the decision (the variable is retained, not removed, so the pinned transition-cleanup sequence and the reset-scope matrix stay byte-identical; it is now the decision's output, no longer its input). The two terrain strips rewind to their top on `new-life` as they already did on `cold-start`/`new-game`, retiring the interim preserve-terrain-on-death fixture. There is no respawn invulnerability window.
- Scratch evidence: the D/G death-trigger hats, the craft-gated `control_if_else` in the `death complete` receiver, and the three-scope terrain rewind in `terrain_blocks`, all in `tools/game_director.py`; the `craft changed` broadcast the triggers send. Contract and negative-fixture checks in `tests/test_scratch_project.py`: `_ply02_failures` (D decrements craft, G drains it, the triggers no longer hardcode an outcome, the death decision is craft-gated with both transitions) with its negatives, and the updated `test_reset_scope_matrix_has_canonical_and_preserving_paths` (terrain now rewinds on `new-life`).
- Acceptance criteria: Pressing **D** runs the explosion and respawns the craft at the fixed point, immediately vulnerable, with the current area restarting from its top; draining the craft (repeated **D**, or **G**) reaches GAME OVER and returns to the title/attract flow (operator). Death caused by a real collision (enemy bullet, flying enemy, or Bacura) is exercised in slice 8, when an attacker exists.
- Fidelity status: PLY-02 is **partial** — the lives-driven respawn-vs-game-over decision, the fixed-point respawn, the no-invulnerability rule, and terrain-restart-on-death are present and operator-verifiable through the D/G fixtures. The real collision **trigger** is blocked until an attacker entity exists (slice 8); the death cue's audio cutoff fidelity is slice 20.
