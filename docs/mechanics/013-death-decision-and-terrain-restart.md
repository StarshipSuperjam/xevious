# Player death decision and terrain-restart-on-death

- Mechanic: PLY-02 (partial) — player death routes through the life economy (respawn while craft remain, else game over) and a new life restarts the current area from its top.
- Derived behavior: When the craft dies, the explosion plays; the game then decides from the remaining craft — a craft left respawns the craft at the fixed spawn point, immediately vulnerable; none left ends the game. On a respawn, the terrain restarts the current area from its top rather than resuming where it was.

The collision hit windows that a real trigger will use are recorded in [008](008-enemy-bullet-foundation.md) (`check_solvalou_hit` 2182-2237). The new-life-restarts-the-area rule is the settled [area progression and terrain](../spec/area-progression-and-terrain.md) reset-scope rule; the death/respawn contract is settled [player craft and weapons](../spec/player-craft-and-weapons.md) PLY-02.
- Transfer class: General behavior.
- Scratch interpretation: The D and G keys are stand-in death **triggers** (a real collision needs an attacker, which arrives in slice 8): D loses one craft, G drains the craft to the terminal life so the game-over path is reachable in one press. Neither hardcodes the outcome. The Stage `death complete` handler decides from the `craft` counter — `craft` > 0 transitions to `respawning`/`new-life`, otherwise to `game-over`/`game-over` — and sets `death outcome` to record the decision (the variable is retained, not removed, so the pinned transition-cleanup sequence and the reset-scope matrix stay byte-identical; it is now the decision's output, no longer its input). The two terrain strips rewind to their top on `new-life` as they already did on `cold-start`/`new-game`, retiring the interim preserve-terrain-on-death fixture. **Slice-20 update (visual terrain, [015](015-area-clock.md)):** the strips no longer rewind on their own; they are drawn from the area clock, so the restart follows from the clock's re-top. Every re-top sets `previous terrain column` to −1 and runs `update terrain` then `terrain draw`, so a new life shows the arcade's forest filler (`xevious_main.68k` `fill_bg_with_forest` 648–669, called at 490 when the life is set up) with the area's first map rows entering above it and scrolling down — the restart band supplies band 3 with its never-written row 255 left as filler. There is no respawn invulnerability window. Since slice 21 (PLY-02.forest-wait, [record 056](056-release-fidelity.md) item 3) the last death, with no craft left, first shows the forest alone for 32 paced frames (the arcade's 64-frame wait, `main_gameplay_loop` 508–546) before the death decision's game-over route or two-player handoff runs.
- Scratch evidence: the D/G death-trigger hats, the craft-gated `control_if_else` in the `death complete` receiver, and the three-scope terrain rewind (since slice 20, the re-top's `update terrain` / `terrain draw` pair, pinned in `_area01_failures` and the harness scenario `terrain-state-follows-clock`), all in `tools/game_director.py`. - Acceptance criteria: Pressing **D** runs the explosion and respawns the craft at the fixed point, immediately vulnerable, with the current area restarting from its top; draining the craft (repeated **D**, or **G**) reaches GAME OVER and returns to the title/attract flow (operator). Death caused by a real collision (enemy bullet, flying enemy, or Bacura) is exercised in slice 8, when an attacker exists.
