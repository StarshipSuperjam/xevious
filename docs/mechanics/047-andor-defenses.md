# Andor Genesis defenses: gun ports, armor, and fire

- Mechanic: The Andor Genesis boss's active defenses once the composite is standing — the four corner gun ports fire player-aimed bullets on the boss fire-frequency mask cadence, the nine armor plates are indestructible, each gun port is individually bombable for 1,000 points, and the destroyed core's converted body flies away as the indestructible Bragza. The core bomb/destruction path, its scoring, the destruction cascade, and the departure animation are the companion mechanic (BOSS-03, [048](048-andor-core-destruction.md)).
- Derived behavior: While the master holds the composite on screen, each of the four gun ports (obj slots 10–13, types 0x4F–0x52) runs a handler with a coroutine reentry (`save_PC_to_fn_tbl_and_ret`): a **one-time init at spawn** sets itself active (`_STATE=2`), sizes 1×1, seeds its fire timer to a fixed `1`, sets its corner `_CODE` and `_PTS=48` (1,000 points), and reads the shared boss fire mask into `_FFREQ`; thereafter, **each frame** it resumes at the tail — positions itself at the master's cell plus its corner offset, copies the shared pulse colour, then **polls the core's state before it fires** and finally services its fire timer (the fixed `1` is seeded once, not re-forced each frame, so the mask cadence governs after the first shot). The core poll is source-exact: the arcade's `cmp.b #3,(core _STATE); jeq <xx>_gun_port_hit` (5523/5574/5625/5676) runs **ahead of** the fire jsr (5533/5584/5635/5686), so on the frame the core dies the port diverts to its own explosion and **fires no extra volley** — and a core-cascaded port keeps `_STATE=2` (active, bombable) throughout that explosion (only `gun_port_explosion_finished`, 5738, sets `_STATE=4` at 5740), so a bomb landing on it mid-burst still scores its 1,000 (the core-destruction cascade is [048](048-andor-core-destruction.md)). Firing itself is gated to every eighth arcade frame: on those frames it decrements `_TIMER`, and when the timer reaches 0 it allocates a bullet from the shared 19-slot bullet pool, spawns it (type 6) at the port's own position aimed once at Solvalou, and reloads `_TIMER = (rng AND _FFREQ) + 1`. The boss fire mask is `47` (`0b101111`), a **non-contiguous** mask: `rng AND 47` yields values in `{0–15, 32–47}` and never `16–31`, so the reload is not a uniform `rng mod 48`. A gun port that is bombed goes to `_STATE=3` through the shared bomb router, awards its 1,000 points once, and runs its explosion tail instead of firing. The nine armor plates (obj slots 1–9, types 0x41–0x49) are each born `_STATE=3` — the reference comments them `indestructible` — and never hit-test themselves; because the shared bomb router only acts on `_STATE==2` objects, armor is skipped by bombs, and the flying-enemy zapper never scans the boss body, so armor is entirely non-interactive and only self-clears when the master goes inactive. Bragza — the body the destroyed core converts into (BOSS-03) — flies as a 1×1, 4-frame animation: its `_CODE` steps `0xb8 + ((countup>>1) & 3)` (codes 0xb8–0xbb) and its `_COLOUR` cycles `0x15 + ((countup>>1) & 7)` (CLUT 0x15–0x1c, not the parts' CLUT 3) while it moves along its stored velocity off the top of the screen.
- Reference provenance: `jotd666/xevious@71473685a8c7856c8401c8519276cd97a38d4183`; `src/xevious_main.68k`: `handle_4F_Andor_Genesis_obj_13` through `handle_52_Andor_Genesis_obj_10` (5508–5687) are the four corner gun ports — each sets `_STATE=2`, clears `_ATTR` (1×1), sets `_TIMER=1`, its corner `_CODE`, `_PTS=48` (1,000), copies the master's position and the shared colour, reads `ffreq_mask_andor_genesis` into `_FFREQ`, dispatches to its `<xx>_gun_port_hit` when its own or the core's `_STATE==3`, and otherwise calls the fire routine; `chk_timer_fire_bullet_reinit_timer` (4999–5010) gates on `countup_timer_1 AND 7`, decrements `_TIMER`, and at 0 fires and reloads `_TIMER = (rng AND _FFREQ) + 1`; `init_new_bullet` (5012–5019) scans the 19-slot shared bullet pool for an idle slot; `found_idle_bullet_slot` (5039–5042) copies the firing object's X/Y and types the bullet `_TYPE=6`; `handle_06_Bullet` (4278–4283) aims a fresh bullet once at Solvalou then flies it straight; `handle_41_Andor_Genesis_obj_09` through `handle_49_Andor_Genesis_obj_01` (5771–5983) are the nine armor plates, each born `_STATE=3` (indestructible), never self-hit-testing, self-clearing only when the master (obj 15) `_STATE==1`; `handle_bombed_obj_and_award_points` (2597–2623) is the 16-object bomb sweep that sets a hit target `_STATE=3` and awards `object_value_tbl[_PTS]`; `check_object_on_target` (2629–2631) hit-tests only `_STATE==2` objects; `handle_Bragza` (5493–5504) flies the converted core with `_CODE=0xb8+((countup>>1)&3)`, `_COLOUR=0x15+((countup>>1)&7)`, and `move_object_dX`. `src/xevious_sub.68k`: `sub_2_fn_22__fire_freq_mask_andor_genesis` (411–412) is the schedule opcode that writes the boss fire mask into `ffreq_mask_andor_genesis`.

- Scratch interpretation: `_ground_seed_andor` (shared by the debug arm and the live schedule) keys every per-slot choice off the slot's `part_type`, never a slot number: each armor slot is seeded to a named `ANDOR_ARMOR_IMMUNE` sentinel state (distinct from both `SLOT_ACTIVE` and `SLOT_HIT`, reusing the ground `SLOT_GARU_BASE` semantics so the active-gated bomb sweep skips it for free); each port is seeded `SLOT_ACTIVE`, `slot pts` = the 1-based `VALUE_TABLE_POINTS` position for 1,000 (offset 48), a `slot fire mask`, and `slot fire timer = 1`. The `update andor part` walk proc, after the unconditional per-frame alignment, calls the shared `install_fire_permission_gate` only when the slot is a port **AND** `SLOT_ACTIVE` **AND** the core slot is not `SLOT_HIT` **AND** the port's own explosion clock `slot timer == 0` — reproducing the arcade's per-port core poll that precedes the fire (so no volley leaves on the core-death frame or once the port is bursting). A directly-bombed port is `SLOT_HIT` (non-active) and stops firing too; a core-cascaded port stays `SLOT_ACTIVE` (bombable) but is suppressed by the core-hit / mid-burst terms — it explodes without firing while remaining alignment-pinned. The fire gate reloads contiguous masks as `(rng mod (mask+1)) + 1`, but a dedicated mask-47 branch reloads bit-exactly as `((rng mod 16) + 32·(floor(rng/32) mod 2)) + 1`, reproducing `rng AND 47`; a generate-time completeness guard fails the build if any fire mask the schedule uses is non-contiguous and not in the handled set. Ports fire through the existing shared 19-slot aimed-bullet pool (0 new render clones); armor renders through the shared ground pool but, being the immune sentinel, is skipped by the `SLOT_ACTIVE`-gated bomb detector, so it is indestructible by construction. Bragza renders as a 4-costume ground family (codes 0xb8–0xbb) selected by `tick & 3` with its cycling colour; its motion belongs to BOSS-03.

- Known deviations or uncertainty: (1) **Bullet aim cadence is approximate.** The arcade aims each bullet once at the player's exact position at birth and services the fire timer every eighth arcade frame; the port fires through the Scratch shared aimed-bullet pool at the tick-rebased cadence, so the aim direction is faithful but the sub-frame timing is the port's `FRAMES_PER_TICK=2` rebasing rather than a frame-exact reproduction. (2) **Mask value provenance.** The boss fire mask (47) is owned by `docs/spec/data/area-schedules.json` (derived from the schedule opcode `sub_2_fn_22__fire_freq_mask_andor_genesis`); the debug arm seeds 47 explicitly so a fresh hold-G playtest exercises the real cadence, because under a bare debug summon no live `fire_mask_andor_genesis` schedule record has run. (3) **Colour is a documented port interpretation**, as in BOSS-01: the arcade indexes a hardware palette byte, and the port maps the same stepped index onto a Scratch `color` graphic effect, approximating rather than reproducing the arcade palette — this applies to Bragza's `0x15`-based cycle as well. (4) The arcade plays firing and explosion sounds each frame; the port emits no sound (audio is a separate slice). (5) When the shared 19-slot bullet pool is full, the port's shot is silently dropped, which is arcade-faithful (the allocator returns no slot). (6) **Debug-summon band protection (port-only guard).** The composite owns its ground band (Scratch slots 2–16) for its whole lifecycle. The debug G summon can drop the boss into whatever area is live, whose schedule may still have pending `add_ground_object` / `add_domogram` records; so the schedule consumer withholds every ground stamp not only while the G key is held but while the invisible master occupies its slot (`andor_boss_present`) — otherwise a stamp landing in the boss's own slots would overwrite the composite one plate at a time, most visibly as it retreats after G is released. This has no arcade equivalent (the arcade has no debug summon) and is a no-op in real play: in areas 4/9/14 every `add_ground_object` record fires above `andor_genesis_start` and has scrolled off before the boss arms, so no schedule ground stamp is ever live while the master is present. The operator pixel-verifies the four Bragza frames at playtest.
