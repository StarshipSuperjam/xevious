# Enemy-bullet pool foundation and collision hit windows

- Mechanic: The enemy-bullet slot allocator and the player-collision hit windows — dormant foundation for AIR-12 (enemy bullets) and PLY-02 (player death), with no live participant this slice.
- Derived behavior: A firer (a later slice) will call `alloc bullet slot` to claim the first idle of the 19 enemy-bullet slots, or get nothing when all 19 are live — the 19-bullet cap; and a later collision detector will test the craft against bullets/flying enemies and Bacura using the two recorded hit windows. Neither has a caller or detector this slice, so no behavior is observable yet.
- Reference provenance: `jotd666/xevious@71473685a8c7856c8401c8519276cd97a38d4183`; the 19-bullet pool (`init_new_bullet`, `find_idle_and_init_radiating_bullet`) and the collision hit windows (`check_solvalou_hit`) are the recorded values in [player craft and weapons](../spec/player-craft-and-weapons.md) (AIR-12, PLY-02). This record cites those homes and does not restate the arcade values; the slot ranges and capacities are recorded in the same document and reproduced as generator constants (with dual arcade-hex/index columns) in `tools/game_director.py`.
- Transfer class: General behavior and numeric constant (instruction-derived structure and recorded window values; no source text or media copied).
- Scratch interpretation: `alloc bullet slot` is a warp (atomic) Stage custom block that sweeps the 19 bullet slots (40-58) with its **own** cursor (`bullet cursor`, deliberately not the shared `slot index`), sets the first idle slot's `slot type`/`slot state` and returns its index in `bullet alloc result` — its OWN result variable, never the blaster's `alloc result`. It mirrors the proven `alloc shot slot` (the 3-shot cap) but over 19 slots via a cursor loop rather than an unrolled block. The dedicated cursor is what lets a future firer call this from inside the `advance slots` per-slot sweep (itself on `slot index`) without corrupting the outer loop — warp atomicity prevents inter-thread preemption, not same-variable reentrancy. The two collision hit windows are recorded as generator constants `HIT_WINDOW_BULLET_FLYING` and `HIT_WINDOW_BACURA` in the reference's half-pixel "shadow" units as `(y_bias, y_width, x_bias, x_width)` — pure recorded data (like `COLLISION_GROUPS`), not emitted into the project, because there is no detector to consume them this slice.
- Scratch evidence: constants `BULLET_TYPE`, `BULLET_ALLOC_RESULT_ID`, `BULLET_CURSOR_ID`, `ALLOC_BULLET_PROCCODE`, `HIT_WINDOW_BULLET_FLYING`, `HIT_WINDOW_BACURA` in `tools/game_director.py`; the `alloc bullet slot` warp custom block (`install_alloc_bullet_slot`, installed on the Stage in `stage_blocks`); the Stage variable `bullet alloc result`; the structural foundation test, its negative fixtures, and the hit-window pin in `tests/test_scratch_project.py` (`_enemy_bullet_pool_failures`, `test_hit_windows_match_spec`).
- Acceptance criteria: The allocator is defined on the Stage, sweeps exactly the 19 bullet slots, marks the bullet occupancy type, uses its own result variable, and is **not called** this slice (dormant); the negative fixtures prove the checks redden when the type or the sweep count is corrupted; the two hit windows equal their recorded values; two clean builds stay byte-identical and survive the build→import round-trip.
- Fidelity status: **Dormant machinery / recorded data, foundation-only.** No enemy fires a bullet, no detector reads the hit windows, and no `slot x/y` position exists yet — the aimed 32-direction vector, the ballistic movement and speed tiers, the screen-edge expiry margins, the four-colour pulse, and the position/vector authoring are all AIR-12 firing behavior owned by the air slice (slice 8), and the collision detection that consumes the windows is owned by slices 4/8. Part of #15. **Slice 8 update:** the allocator is now live — the shooting Toroid calls `alloc bullet slot` to fire one aimed enemy bullet, the bullet flies and culls, and the bullet/flying craft-hit window now detects real overlaps ([record 026](026-enemy-bullets-and-collision-death.md)); the four-colour pulse and dedicated crops remain deferred.
- License status: The pinned reference repository states no reusable license; no reference source text or media was copied — the allocator structure is instruction-derived and the window values are a derived transcription.
- Known deviations or uncertainty: (1) The 19-slot range, its capacity, and the enemy-shots-vs-player collision group **already shipped in #14** (the entity-slot foundation); this slice adds only the allocator and the hit-window data — it does not re-count what #14 delivered. (2) `alloc bullet slot` may be **revised in slice 8** if aimed bullets seed a position or vector at allocation time (unlike the straight-flying player shot, whose clone self-positions), so its current shape is not frozen. (3) The hit windows are in **half-pixel shadow units**; the collision slice must convert shadow→pixel and then apply the port scale — the unit is labeled here so that conversion is not lost. (4) The allocator uses its **own** cursor (`bullet cursor`), not the shared slot-sweep cursor, so a firer may call it from inside the slot sweep without corrupting the outer loop.
